About me

I am a Machine Learning Engineer at TikTok, Singapore. I received my Ph.D. degree in Civil Engineering (Transportation) from The University of Hong Kong and earned a Bachelor degree in Civil Engineering from Tsinghua University. I was also a visiting Ph.D. student at University of Wisconsin–Madison for academic exchange. I also interned at JD.com, Baidu, and XPeng. My current research interests include LLM, embodied intelligence, autonomous driving, shared mobility services, and civil engineering informatics. Please feel free to drop me an email for collaboration, or chat.
